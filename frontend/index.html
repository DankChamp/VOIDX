How We Work

Principles over process.

No standups for the sake of standups. No tickets without context. These aren't aspirational — they're how we operate daily.

Trust by Default

No permission gates. Ship to main. If it breaks, we fix it together. Autonomy requires accountability.

Write It Down

Decisions live in docs, not Slack. RFCs > meetings. Async-first means anyone can contribute on their schedule.

Iterate in Private

Explore ugly. Prototype fast. Polish before public. The workspace is a sandbox — production is the stage.

People First

Burnout isn't a badge. Rest is productive. We optimize for long-term velocity, not sprint bursts.

Join Us
